i InTempo · Design preview
v0.7 · 2026-04-25 · refined pendulum

Linear-leaning aesthetic with warmth. Charcoal text on a near-white warm background, generous whitespace, subtle borders instead of shadows, and a single deep-amber accent that does the brand work. Verdict colors (green / amber / red) are quarantined to the verdict UI only — they never appear in chrome, navigation, or buttons elsewhere. Verdicts are words, not numbers — "Slight rush" and "Rushing" tell a musician what happened; the bar visualization shows how much. Below: nine reference screens covering the full app flow — splash, library, score capture, tempo selector, recording, analyzing, verdict, per-measure drill-down, and paywall.

Motion

Default: 200ms ease-out · for hovers, taps, layout shifts.
Verdict reveal: 400ms spring (stiffness 220, damping 26).
Stagger: 40ms between sibling reveals (per-measure verdicts).
Reduced motion: all animations become instant; opacity only.
Principles
Do
  • Solid amber accent for primary CTAs, focused inputs, and brand moments.
  • Borders at 1px, near-white background #FAFAF7 not pure white.
  • Verdicts as words: "On tempo" / "Slight rush" / "Rushing" / "Slight drag" / "Dragging".
  • Bars and colors show magnitude. The word tells you what; the visual tells you how much.
  • Numbers (BPM offsets, milliseconds) are dev-mode only. Never in the production UI.
  • Tabular numerals on the few numbers that do appear (timer, target BPM).
  • Generous padding inside cards (16–20px) and between sections (24–32px).
  • Verdict color + icon + text label every time the colors appear.
  • One typeface (Geist Sans) at 4–5 sizes max.
Don't
  • No gradients anywhere except the verdict reveal background.
  • No drop shadows on any UI element.
  • No emoji as UI icons — Lucide or Phosphor only.
  • No glassmorphism, blur effects, or animated backgrounds.
  • No verdict colors outside the verdict UI.
  • No more than one accent color visible per screen.
