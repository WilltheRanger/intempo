# Instrument recordings

VSCO 2 Community Edition by Sam Gossner / Versilian Studios and Simon Dalzell,
with sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote. Dedicated to the public domain
under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
Each source path, pinned revision and author-provided MIDI/tuning mapping is in
provenance.json. Regenerate with `node scripts/prepare-instrument-samples.mjs`.
Derived assets are mono 16-bit PCM, peak-normalized, leading silence trimmed,
and capped at five seconds. No proprietary Muse Sounds assets are included.
Violin and double bass are solo recordings; viola and cello are sections.
