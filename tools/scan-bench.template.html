The page

Drop a photograph here, or click to choose one.
One line of music reads best — that is what the prompt asks for. HEIC straight off an iPhone is fine.
Your key is sent to the model provider and nowhere else — this page has no backend. It is kept in this browser’s localStorage so you don’t retype it, and never leaves the machine except in the request you just made. Costs are real: roughly $0.005 a page on Flash, $0.03 on Sonnet.

Photographs are re-encoded before they are sent: HEIC becomes JPEG, anything over 2000px is scaled down, and EXIF rotation is applied to the pixels so a portrait shot doesn’t arrive sideways. A 12-megapixel phone photo goes from about 3 MB to 30 KB, which the models would have downsampled anyway and which you would otherwise have been billed for. HEIC needs Safari to convert; on Chrome or Firefox it is passed to Gemini as-is, which accepts the format — Claude does not.
