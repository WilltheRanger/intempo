Beats are quarter-notes, matching alignment.py, so 6/8 is three beats and not six. A first measure that comes up short is allowed — that is a pickup, not a mistake. A meter is only inferred from the music when at least 60% of three or more measures agree, because the most common of several different answers is not a majority.
